Community support is best effort. A public question or issue does not create a support service agreement. The self-hosted core remains free without a paid feature tier.

## Make a report useful and safe to share

Include the release or commit, deployment method, relevant AI provider configuration, steps to reproduce, expected outcome and actual outcome. If a result looks accessible but changes the meaning of source content, explain the discrepancy and whether it is visible in the saved output.

Use synthetic or anonymised examples. Remove student records, names, credentials, tokens, private URLs and identifying metadata from documents, screenshots and logs before posting. Do not attach institutional course material unless you have permission to publish it. Report suspected vulnerabilities privately even if the reproduction appears small.
